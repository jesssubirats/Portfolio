Reporting & analytics

The layer the organization forecasts on

GeneID Analytics Dashboard

Built the reporting layer myself rather than waiting on a data team. Pulls submissions, insurance verification, and lab approvals into one view, then breaks performance down by state, account manager, account, and individual specialist. Includes an outreach calendar, per-account drill-downs with qualification counts and field notes, and an Insights-only mode for isolating the AI product's contribution.

This is what replaced the manual spreadsheet roll-up, and it is where the forecast now comes from.
